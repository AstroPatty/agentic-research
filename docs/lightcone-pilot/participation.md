# Participating in the Lightcone Research Pilot

The [Lightcone Research Pilot](README.md) brings DESC scientists together with the Lightcone team to test and improve its agentic tooling on scientific workflows. The team, made up of DESC members, provides focused engineering, compute, and model inference. **Resources are limited, and the goal is a focused effort with fast turnaround.** The team therefore aims to extend dedicated support to a limited number of projects where close scientific collaboration can help evaluate the tools and make useful progress quickly.

This process applies only to participation in the Lightcone pilot through **Lightcone Research Pilot Proposals**. Everyone is welcome to experiment with other tools, develop workflows, and collaborate independently. Bring broader research ideas to [General](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/categories/general), share experience in [Show & Tell](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/categories/show-and-tell), or join [Office Hours](../office-hours.md); none requires participation in the Lightcone pilot.

## What matters

1. **Scientific value:** Does the question address an important scientific challenge or bottleneck?
2. **Engagement of the scientific lead:** Will a researcher help scope, try, and validate the tools, and provide feedback on results and limitations? Availability for active, hands-on scientific collaboration is a major consideration.
3. **Opportunity to evaluate Lightcone's tooling:** Could the workflow help reveal where the tools are useful, where they fall short, and what needs improvement? No prior knowledge of the tools is required; the team can help explore the fit.
4. **Feasibility and resources:** Is there a sensible first milestone, and are the required data, infrastructure, people, and resources realistically accessible?
5. **Reusable value:** Can results and lessons help other DESC scientists or improve open, inspectable research workflows?

These questions help researchers and the Lightcone team find a useful scope for the available effort. There is no numerical scoring formula or popularity ranking.

## Process

1. The [call for ideas is now open](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/2), with proposals due by **16 October 2026**. Submit your idea using the [Lightcone pilot proposal form](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/new?category=lightcone-research-pilot-proposals). See the [Lightcone pilot overview](README.md) for the activity's scope.
2. DESC members can comment, offer to collaborate, flag related efforts, and endorse proposals. **Upvotes signal interest; they are not a ranking or mandate.**
3. The Lightcone team and scientific collaborators discuss the research aim, a useful first test of the tooling, how to validate it, collaborators' availability, and the support the team can offer. Organizers may seek clarification or suggest combining related proposals.
4. When the team and scientific collaborators agree on a project and its support, organizers apply the `selected` label, explain the agreed focus, and identify a contact and scoping plan. The plan is to propose these projects as sprints at **DESC Sprint Week**, where the Lightcone Research Pilot will kick off with DESC members. Support remains subject to capacity, permissions, and institutional procedures. Actual projects are added to [the Lightcone pilot project list](projects.md).
5. When dedicated support is unavailable, organizers explain briefly whether capacity, timing, readiness, or fit is the limiting factor. Researchers remain welcome to pursue the idea, find collaborators, share progress, and bring questions to [Office Hours](../office-hours.md).

The `proposal`, `under-review`, `selected`, `not-selected-this-round`, and `deferred` labels track the status of dedicated Lightcone support. They do not determine who can participate in the community. Keep the original discussion and its disposition visible, subject to privacy and moderation needs. Leave threads open when dedicated support is unavailable so discussion and collaboration can continue.

Explain decisions without publishing private personnel evaluations, confidential deliberations, or private communications. Transparency means understandable criteria and decisions.

## Separate DESC approvals

An agreement for Lightcone support does not approve a formal DESC Project or replace working-group review. If work in this pilot develops into a formal scientific or infrastructure Project, coordinate with the relevant working groups and follow the standard DESC project announcement and approval process. Publication, authorship, source releases, licensing, data sharing, and access for external collaborators follow the relevant [DESC policies](https://lsstdesc.org/pages/policies.html) and institutional procedures. Review external-collaborator requirements before non-DESC participants receive non-public resources.

# Agentic Research @ LSST DESC

This repository is a shared home for agentic research within the LSST Dark Energy Science Collaboration (DESC). It brings together scientists interested in exploring how AI agents can support research, exchanging practical experience, and developing tools and practices for transparent, reproducible science.

We welcome ideas, workflows, and community activities from across DESC. This space can support multiple initiatives and collaborations as members explore new scientific questions and ways of working.

## Get involved

**Discuss research ideas.** Use [General](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/categories/general) to explore scientific questions, find collaborators, and suggest activities. You do not need prior agentic AI experience or a fully designed solution to join the conversation.

**Join Agentic AI Office Hours.** These planned informal sessions are open to DESC members interested in agentic research. Bring questions about tools, workflows, evaluation, or research ideas. [Learn about office hours](docs/office-hours.md) and ask in [Office Hours & Questions](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/categories/office-hours-questions). Session details are to be announced.

**Share what you learn.** Use [Show & Tell](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/categories/show-and-tell) for useful tools, demonstrations, experiences, and lessons from your research.

## First activity: Lightcone Research Pilot

The **Lightcone Research Pilot** is a first activity within this broader community. Participating DESC members will test and help improve **Lightcone Research's agentic tooling** on real LSST science questions and workflows. The Lightcone Research team brings focused engineering, compute, and model inference to this work, with the aim of understanding where its tools are scientifically useful and developing best practices the Collaboration can adopt more broadly.

Because resources are limited and the aim is a focused effort with fast turnaround, the Lightcone team plans to extend this dedicated support to a limited number of projects, subject to capacity. **Everyone is welcome to experiment, develop workflows, share what they learn, and collaborate**, whether or not their project receives this support.

The call for ideas is planned for **12 October 2026**, with proposals due by **16 October 2026**. We plan to initiate participating projects as sprints at **DESC Sprint Week**, where we will kick off the Lightcone Research Pilot with DESC members.

[Read about the Lightcone Research Pilot](docs/lightcone-pilot/README.md). The [Lightcone Research Pilot Proposals](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/categories/lightcone-research-pilot-proposals) category is for research ideas that could help test and improve Lightcone's tooling through this focused collaboration. The official call is pending approval. Broader research ideas and other initiatives are welcome in [General](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/categories/general).

[Read how to participate in the Lightcone pilot](docs/lightcone-pilot/participation.md) and see [participating projects](docs/lightcone-pilot/projects.md).
